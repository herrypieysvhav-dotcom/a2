Culinary Pillar & Ritual

The Four Ceremonial Steaming Stages

Authentic couscous is never boiled like pasta or rice. It is coaxed into airy, cloud-like perfection through rhythmic moisture sprinkling and fragrant vapor filtration.

Stage 01 // Hand Craft

Hand-Rolled Semolina

Coarse and fine durum wheat middlings are hand-rolled across wide woven esparto sieves with salted water droplets until each grain achieves calibrated spherical form.

Stage 02 // Vapor Aeration

Hammered Copper Kesskess

Suspended above aromatic simmering broths, the perforated upper tier allows spice-infused vapor to cook the grains without soaking, expanding each bead to three times its volume.

Stage 03 // Botanical Alchemy

Aged Smen & 27 Spices

Between steaming cycles, the grains are turned in wide terracotta platters, raked with fingertips, and enriched with cultured sheep's butter and cold-pressed Picholine olive oil.

Stage 04 // Hospitality

Ceremonial Mint Tea

Every communal feast concludes with the traditional high pour of fresh spearmint tea and orange blossom water, cleansing the palate and honoring visiting guests.

Heirloom Ingredients

The architecture of our 24-hour simmered master broth

A truly transcendent couscous dinner depends entirely on the equilibrium of its master broth. In our New York atelier at 181 Mercer Street, broad copper cauldrons simmer for hours with tender cuts of grass-fed lamb shoulder, sweet marrow bones, and heirloom chickpeas soaked in mountain mineral water.

Heirloom purple-top turnips, sweet Mediterranean parsnips, butternut squash, and summer zucchini are layered sequentially by cooking density, releasing rich caramelized sugars that perfume the billowing steam rising into the couscous tier above.

Finished with Saffron Tfaya Confit Sweet caramelized onions stewed with cinnamon bark, golden sultanas, and pure Taliouine saffron stigmas.
