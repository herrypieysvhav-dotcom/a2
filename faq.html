Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our communal dining traditions, grain milling, and dietary options.

Why is authentic couscous steamed three separate times?

Unlike factory-made instant couscous which has been pre-cooked, pressed, and dehydrated, authentic couscous consists of raw stone-ground durum wheat middlings. Steaming it in three progressive stages allows the starch granules to gelatinize gradually while remaining completely separate. Between each steaming round, the grains are sprinkled with cold salted water and fluffed by hand with olive butter to aerate the matrix, producing a texture that is light as down and never gummy.

Can you accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and halal dining requests?

Absolutely. All meats served at Couscous Party are certified 100% pasture-raised and halal. For our vegetarian and vegan guests, we maintain dedicated terracotta couscoussier steamers and separate copper cauldrons for our Atlas Mountain Garden Feast, simmered with seven root vegetables, sweet parsnips, and chickpeas in an olive-oil saffron broth completely free of animal stocks.

What is Smen and how is it utilized in the dish?

Smen is a traditional Maghrebi aged clarified butter cultured with sea salt and wild mountain oregano. Aged in sealed terracotta jars for months to years, it develops a pungent, earthy complexity similar to aged blue cheese or dry-cured ham. Just a single teaspoon folded into the warm steamed grains imparts the unmistakable deep umami signature characteristic of authentic royal Moroccan banqueting.

How is communal dining practiced at Couscous Party?

In accordance with ancient Amazigh hospitality, our feasts are centered around massive round hand-painted ceramic platters known as gsa'a. Guests are seated around the platter and served fragrant broth in individual bowls. In the traditional manner, each guest eats from their section of the platter, enjoying the harmonious mingling of meat, root vegetables, sweet onion tfaya, and aerated grain.

What is the cancellation policy for private banquet reservations?

Because our slow-braised broths, hand-rolled grains, and butchered meats are pre-ordered and prepared 48 hours in advance, private dining salon reservations require 72 hours' advance notice for complimentary cancellation or date rescheduling. For parties larger than eight guests, please refer to our formal banquet terms or contact our concierge.
