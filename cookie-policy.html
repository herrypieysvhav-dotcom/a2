COOKIE POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Couscous Party Flagship Dining Salon

1. Definition and Technical Purpose of Cookies

Cookies represent small text data files deposited onto your computer, tablet, or mobile browser when visiting modern websites, allowing the web server to recognize your device across browsing sessions. Couscous Party utilizes cookies and local browser storage tokens to preserve reservation progress, remember language preferences, and ensure seamless encrypted table bookings. Questions regarding our cookie deployment may be directed to our technical administrative staff at +1-888-777-5845 or submitted in writing to our salon at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.

2. Strictly Necessary Core Operational Cookies

Essential cookies are technically mandatory to allow baseline website navigation and activate secure platform functionality, including encrypted payment tokenization and reservation session validation. Without these critical data cookies, our digital dinner menus, table booking engines, and banquet scheduling forms cannot function reliably. These core operational files do not gather marketing profiling intelligence, nor do they track your browsing history across external domains, operating strictly to maintain essential website security and infrastructural stability.

3. Performance and Aggregate Analytics Cookies

Performance cookies gather aggregated, anonymous statistical data regarding how visitors interact with our digital platform, identifying frequently read research monographs, navigation drop-off points, and page load latency. We implement Google Analytics tag G-0LY0HY7L01 using strict IP address anonymization to safeguard visitor privacy while evaluating overall server health and interface usability. This aggregate analytical intelligence enables our web engineering team to continuously improve content layout, optimize technical document accessibility, and enhance mobile browsing responsiveness.

4. Functional and Preference Storage Cookies

Functional cookies enable our platform to recall customized choices you establish during browsing, including preferred party size defaults, dietary filter selections, and regional currency configurations. By preserving your individual operational preferences, functional cookies eliminate the inconvenience of repeatedly reconfiguring display options upon subsequent visits to our online salon. These cookies remain active for a maximum duration of twelve months unless manually purged from your local web browser cache earlier.

5. Third-Party Integration and External Telemetry

Select third-party enterprise partners, including verified reservation scheduling platforms and content delivery networks, may deploy independent session cookies to execute authenticated transaction tokens. We enforce strict contractual oversight ensuring that third-party vendors access only the minimal technical data required to securely authorize reservations and mitigate automated fraud attempts. We maintain zero integration with predatory third-party advertising retargeting networks, ensuring your personal browsing habits are never commercialized or tracked across third-party websites.

6. User Control and Browser Cookie Management

You preserve the absolute legal right to manage, restrict, or entirely disable the storage of cookies on your personal devices by configuring the privacy settings within your web browser. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may degrade website navigation, prevent table reservation completion, or restrict access to specialized banquet customization forms. For detailed guidance on configuring cookie permissions or requesting data assistance, contact our technical team at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our verified support line directly at +1-888-777-5845.
