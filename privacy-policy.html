PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Couscous Party Flagship Dining Salon

1. Data Controller and Institutional Identification

Couscous Party operates as the primary data controller responsible for the lawful, transparent processing of personal records collected through our digital interfaces and flagship dining salon located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. Patrons requiring assistance regarding reservation records, dietary logs, or formal privacy inquiries may establish direct contact with our administrative compliance team by telephone at +1-888-777-5845 during regular operating hours. We uphold comprehensive technical safeguards to protect all personal records against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or unlawful destruction across all customer communication touchpoints worldwide.

2. Scope of Collected Information

We collect personal information strictly necessary to coordinate table reservations, process private banquet bookings, and customize culinary dining experiences according to guest preferences. Such data includes guest names, electronic mail addresses, telephone numbers, billing coordinates, and dietary allergy specifications submitted through our reservation portal. We do not aggregate irrelevant demographic metrics, nor do we acquire extraneous personal metadata from secondary commercial data brokers without your explicit documented authorization and verifiable affirmative consent.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

All processing operations conducted by our salon rely upon established legal grounds under international data protection frameworks, including the execution of dining contracts and legitimate commercial interests. Specifically, customer details are processed to confirm seating availability, send reservation reminders, accommodate medical dietary restrictions, and facilitate event invoicing. Where required by governing privacy regulations, explicit affirmative consent is obtained prior to transmitting seasonal menu notifications, culinary white papers, or cultural banquet invitations to your designated email addresses.

4. Third-Party Disclosures and Service Partners

Personal records are transmitted exclusively to vetted enterprise service providers essential for operating our dining establishment, including table reservation management software, encrypted payment processing gateways, and secure cloud servers. Each partnering vendor is bound by rigorous contractual data processing covenants that strictly prohibit secondary data exploitation, commercial profiling, or unauthorized external sharing beyond executing specific hospitality tasks. We never sell, lease, or monetize client personal data to marketing exchanges, advertising syndicates, or commercial data aggregators under any circumstances whatsoever.

5. Data Retention Schedules and Security

Client personal records are preserved only for the duration necessary to satisfy the hospitality purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory legal, tax, and accounting mandates. Standard dining reservation archives and billing receipts are maintained securely within encrypted server facilities for a duration of seven years following account inactivity to satisfy fiscal audit standards. Upon the expiration of statutory retention intervals, all identifiable personal data is permanently scrubbed from our systems through certified cryptographic erasure protocols and physical storage degaussing procedures.

6. Data Subject Rights and Formal Channels

Under applicable data privacy statutes, you maintain comprehensive entitlements to request access to your personal files, rectify factual inaccuracies, demand complete data erasure, or restrict processing activities at any time. To exercise these statutory privileges, or to lodge formal privacy questions, please submit formal correspondence to our legal department at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or dial our verified telephone support line at +1-888-777-5845. We evaluate and respond to all verified identity requests within thirty calendar days in full accordance with enforceable procedural standards.
